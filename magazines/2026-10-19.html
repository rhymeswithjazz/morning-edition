Hybrid Reasoning Agents: LLM agents that think in latent space, act through text

by allinonai · 473 points · 225 comments

This paper proposes agents that do their *reasoning* in continuous latent space (skipping the token-by-token chain-of-thought overhead) but still act through normal text outputs — essentially combining the speed of neural-implicit reasoning with the interpretability of language actions. It's a credible path toward faster, cheaper agentic loops, directly relevant to anyone building or using Claude Code-style tools. The HN thread unpacks the architecture tradeoffs with unusual depth.
